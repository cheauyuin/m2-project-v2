Chapter 01 — Demand Patterns

Two Markets, One Fleet

Weekdays are shaped by commuters — sharp spikes at 8am and 6pm. Weekends are flat and leisure-driven. Toggle between them to see two completely different demand profiles sharing the same bikes.

Chapter 02 — Station Flow

The Redistribution Problem

Some stations persistently drain bikes (sources); others overflow (sinks). Vans must constantly rebalance the network. Click a marker to inspect any station. Use the filter to isolate the worst offenders.

🔴 Source (net outflow) 🔵 Sink (net inflow) ⚫ Balanced · Marker size = imbalance magnitude
Chapter 03 — Seasonality

Eight Years of Seasons

A 3× swing between summer peaks and winter lows. COVID lockdowns triggered a leisure surge in 2020. The Queen's death caused a measurable dip in September 2022. Hover disrupted months to read the event label.

Chapter 04 — Nightlife Segment

The Third Market: Nightlife

Saturday midnight sees 2.3× more rentals than Tuesday — a distinct third market invisible in aggregate stats. The map shows where to pre-position bikes before midnight on Friday.
